# Shot list and asset needs: The Entire History of Tata (v4 narration, 20:57)

*Creative director's plan for the sourcing session (PLAYBOOK §11) and the scene builders (§10.3). Asset IDs are the
`id` column of `films/tata-history/media_manifest.csv`. Real before made (§6.3.6): archive photos and documents first,
licensed stock second, our own animation and 3D third, AI never of a real person. Timings come from
`films/tata-history/vo/timing.json`.*

## Art direction (inside the brand world, §6.1)
- **One white-hot point of light:** the Jubilee diamond. Nothing else in the film glows white.
- **Colour by era:** furnace orange and soot (1868–1924), sepia and aviation blue (J.R.D., 1929–1978), steel grey and
  boardroom glass (Ratan, 1991–2012), the Taj night in ember and smoke, cleanroom cyan for now (2012–2026).
- **Texture:** paper and ledger for money, blueprint grid for machines, film grain everywhere, dust in light beams.
- **Archive photos always move** (2.5D parallax, push-ins); documents sit on the isometric desk with a highlighter that
  follows the voice; dates and places as engraved stamps ("BOMBAY · 1924").
- **INK figures** (bronze line on charcoal, §8.9) for the anonymous people no camera caught: the workers waiting for
  wages, the 1907 investor queue, the family of four on a scooter. Never a named person.

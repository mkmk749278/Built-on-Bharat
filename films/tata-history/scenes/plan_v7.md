# Picture plan v7: one world, all of it real (film 1, 2026-10-28)

*Why v7:* the owner shared an outside review of the v6 cut (narration 9.5/10, visuals 5.5/10: "five design universes
colliding") and said the film "is not that mark". The session's own audit of the cut (`review/visual_audit_v7.md`, one
frame every 3 s) agrees and finds the deeper cause. v7 replaces the picture grammar of `plan_v6.md` §3; the VO, timing,
facts, sound anchors and the shots that already work stay as they are.

## 1. The diagnosis in one line
The best shots in the cut are **grounded**: a real place, a real photograph, a real machine, a real number. The worst
are **a thing floating in a dark void**: a drawn figure, a print on an empty grid, a text card, a blank postcard, the
same Blender box again. The void is the template look. v7 removes the void.

## 2. The rules of v7 (for this film and the channel)
1. **No void.** Every frame has a real-world base: archive photo (2.5D depth), real footage, a satellite plate, or one
   of the two built worlds below. Typography, counters and stamps ride *on* that base, never on black.
2. **No drawn people.** INK figures are retired from film 1 (and from the channel's documentary style): anonymous
   people come from real photographs (Commons, LoC, Openverse), never drawings, never AI. *Why:* thin line figures read
   as an app explainer; on 26/11 they read as detached (outside review; our audit agrees).
3. **Two built worlds only, each with one look:**
   - **The archive table** (`ArchiveTable`): one physically lit desk (Cycles plate: dark oiled teak, a green leather
     blotter, brass lamp pool, out-of-focus papers) where every document, quote, letter, ledger and calendar lives, in
     true perspective with a contact shadow, paper grain and the lamp's falloff. Replaces the grid "Desk".
   - **The machine room** (technical drawings): the furnace cutaway and the dividend bar (both praised), the new
     ownership flow (glass cards and bus lines, same family as the dividend bar), the TCS bar. Blueprint on charcoal.
4. **Real ground for every map** (`SatMap`): Copernicus Sentinel-2 true colour (our own cloud-free median, 10 m) and the
   Copernicus 30 m DEM for relief, NASA Blue Marble for all-India views, India's official boundary drawn on top
   (PLAYBOOK §6.6). Pins and routes are thin bronze lines on real terrain. Credit: "Contains modified Copernicus
   Sentinel data <year>". (EOX s2cloudless is NC: never.)
5. **Portraits are plates, not prints.** A portrait fills the frame's height on one side with its own depth parallax;
   the other side is a real place from the same story (Navsari, Bombay House, the mill) in soft focus. No cream border
   on a void. **Each photograph at most twice in the film**, the second time only as a deliberate callback.
6. **The diamond three times, never as filler:** the cold open (one continuous scene, 0:04–0:24), the 1924 pledge
   (9:40–10:05), and the last image of the film. Every other diamond cut becomes a real photograph or document.
7. **Pace:** a visual change every ~3.5 s; no single image over ~6 s without a new move or a new layer.
8. **One film stock:** every source passes through one finishing stack (grade by era, halation, grain, gate weave on
   archive, matched blacks), so archive, satellite, footage and 3D read as one film.
9. **Third-party news:** horizontal only, no channel bugs or burned-in captions in frame (crop or skip).

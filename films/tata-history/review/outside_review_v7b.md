## 3. What we don't take
**Facts we can't source, or that are wrong** (they would break PLAYBOOK §9):
- "The 5th largest diamond on Earth"; "to save thousands from starvation" (the film says wages, which is what's on record).
- "Pensions and accident insurance before European labour laws existed" (Germany had accident insurance by 1884).
- "Tiger-infested hills", "a decade-long expedition", "Bose leading Dorabji into the hills".
- "Their entire personal estate"; "21 pieces laid out before the bank officers" (the 21 pieces are in the 1932 will, not
  in the 1924 pledge).
- "Ford on the brink of bankruptcy" (2008); "66%" (we say about two thirds, as sourced); "IISc metallurgy labs funded by
  the trusts" (no source).
- A "1895 company circular": it was a speech. The on-screen text matches our source word for word (T18).

**Evidence it asks us to fake** (CLAUDE.md honesty rules):
- An "authentic" 1920s appraisal with British bank seals; "archival film of Empress Mills workers, smiling"; "J.R.D.
  walking alone across the tarmac"; "1912 footage of the tap hole". Each can go in only if a real one is found, or else
  as a labelled ILLUSTRATION.
- **Ratan Tata's face reflected in the plane window.** That builds an image of a real person in a moment no one
  photographed. Never.
- **The tricolour beside the Union Jack, "the colony now owns the crown jewel".** Jingoistic. That isn't our voice
  (PLAYBOOK §4).
- **"Tata is not an empire that extracts from India; it is an engine that feeds India."** That's advertorial. The film
  earns its trust by also telling the bill (Corus, the Nano, Mistry).
- "Robotic arms in the Dholera fab." The fab isn't built, so no frame may claim it is.

## 5. Lesson for the playbook
An outside AI review is a strong eye for the picture and a weak source of facts: its knowledge can predate events in
our film (it called AI171 a "hallucination"), and its "fixes" add drama the record doesn't hold. Take its diagnosis;
put every fact it adds and every image it invents through §9 like any other source.

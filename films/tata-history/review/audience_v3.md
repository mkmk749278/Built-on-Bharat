## Panel: top 7 problems, ranked

**1. The middle runs backwards in time and sags.** The licence-raj passage comes after 1978, then jumps back to 1968.
- **Now:** "These are hard years for big business. A company needs the government's permission to grow… But in nineteen sixty eight, almost quietly, Tata had started a small computer unit."
- **Fix:** Move it before "April, nineteen seventy four" and say: "In these years, a company needs the government's permission to grow. What it makes. How much. So Tata grows slowly. But in nineteen sixty eight, almost quietly, it starts a small computer unit."

**2. Words that Ramesh and Fatima lose on first hearing.**
- "He's here to borrow against it." → "He's here to get a loan, with the diamond as guarantee."
- "The banks want security." → "The banks want something valuable as a guarantee."
- "Old and young, rich and poor, men and women, they came, offering their mites." → "Old and young, rich and poor, men and women, they came with their small savings."
- "Iron ore, coke made from coal, and limestone go in at the top." → "Iron ore, a kind of baked coal called coke, and limestone go in at the top."

**3. YouTuber filler and too many rhetorical questions.** Priya and Arjun both checked out here.
- "I'll show you how, by the end. I promise it's worth the wait. But first, ask yourself this." → "By the end of this film, you'll see how."
- "Can you imagine that flight? Hold on to that silence." → "That was a long, quiet flight home." Also cut either "Would you have done it?" or "What would you do?"

**4. The ending is abstract just before the emotional close.** This is where Fatima swipes away.
- **Now:** "Tata Sons asked the R.B.I. to let it stay out of certain rules. The R.B.I. said no. And those rules push Tata Sons towards the stock market."
- **Fix:** "Big companies like Tata Sons have to follow strict RBI rules. Tata Sons asked to be let off. The RBI said no. And under those rules, Tata Sons may have to sell its shares to the public, for the very first time."

**5. Dates that blur when you hear them.**
- "In September, the Tata Sons board voted…" → "This September, the Tata Sons board voted…"
- "Because the choice this man makes, eight years from now…" → "Because the choice this man makes, eight years later…"
- "In the last financial year, it brought in about eighty seven percent…" → "Last year, about eighty seven percent of all the dividend money Tata Sons got, came from T.C.S. alone."

**6. Lines that sound like exaggeration or a defensive tone.**
- "More than twice the size of the Kohinoor." → "More than twice the size of the Kohinoor as it is today."
- "Real people, with real demands about their pay and their lives." → "They want better pay and better lives."
- "So the money would serve India." → "So the money would go to charity, not to his family."

**7. Too many names for a phone listener, and TTS risk.**
- "Jehangir Ratanji Dadabhoy Tata. Everyone calls him J.R.D." → "His full name is long. Everyone just calls him J.R.D."
- "A man who saw it, Axel Sahlin, wrote it down." → "One man who was there wrote it down."
- Write J.R.D., T.C.S. and R.B.I. phonetically in the voice script (JRD, TCS, RBI), and check how "leukaemia", "Meherbai" and "Puss Moth" come out before the final render.
